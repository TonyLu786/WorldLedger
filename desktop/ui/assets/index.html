WorldLedger for Minecraft

Set up

WorldLedger keeps the world your Minecraft client is already being shown, so you can go back to it later. This checks that everything it needs is in place.

Play

Join a server and play normally. What your client is shown is kept as you go, without touching the server or the world.

Bring it in

Everything kept while you played is waiting to be added to your archive. Your recordings stay where they are as well — nothing is deleted.

Decide what may be shared

An archive holds where you went and when. Nothing can be made into a world until someone has said what may happen to it. This is the one step that is not done for you.

Make a world

Your recordings are written into a world Minecraft made. A world’s seed and rules were never yours to see, so one is never invented — you make an empty world, and this fills in what you saw.
